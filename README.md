## 💼 Production Freelance & Client Projects

### ⚡ 01. Hyperlocal Service Marketplace
`Marketplace · Realtime · Micro-Services` · *Client Project*

> **On-demand hyperlocal service platform connecting customers to nearby service providers with real-time tracking, peer-to-peer voice calling, and automated checkout — without exposing personal contact details.**

- **Problem:** Connecting customers to nearby verified service providers with live tracking and payment in one seamless flow, without either party sharing raw phone numbers or personal contact info.
- **Solution:** A scalable hyperlocal marketplace featuring geospatial provider discovery, live GPS job dispatching, encrypted in-app voice calling, and an automated payment lifecycle.
- **Key Highlights:**
  - **Geospatial Discovery:** MongoDB `2dsphere` indexes for radius-based provider matching and low-latency proximity queries.
  - **Live Dispatch & GPS:** `Socket.io` channels scoped per active job for real-time location streaming and provider availability tracking.
  - **Privacy-Preserving Audio:** In-app **WebRTC voice calling** ensuring customer and provider connect directly without exchanging phone numbers.
  - **Automated Settlement:** End-to-end **Razorpay** payment flow with signature verification and webhook reconciliation for instant payout triggering.
- **Tech Stack:** `Next.js` · `MongoDB` · `Razorpay` · `Socket.io` · `WebRTC` · `Node.js`
